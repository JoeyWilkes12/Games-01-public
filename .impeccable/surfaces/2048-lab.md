# 2048 lab

Mode: Operate, with reading as a secondary task.

Visitor success: compare recorded strategies within one board, inspect the supporting runs, and understand how published research relates to those observations.

Flow: board selection → score comparison → spread and board context → run inspection → source commentary. Three URL-addressable tabs keep phone pages focused; changing boards updates all dependent results.

Direction: a puzzle and cognitive-science exhibit. The mechanism is visible in the game tiles and in inspectable evidence. Keep a cool daylight ground, quiet data surfaces, precise controls, and a single rotated 2048 tile as a link back to play. The hub leads with a tile board, not a stock icon.

Constraints: source fields only; no invented time, moves, maximum tiles or win rates. Preserve original source rows and notes. No deployment changes.
