Logic puzzles, lucky rolls, and the ideas that make the next move. Game Hub brings browser games and a closer look at algorithmic strategy into one place.

Play a little

  • 2048: join matching tiles, make room, and explore the built-in Expectimax solver.
  • Color Match: slide tiles into a target pattern, in color or number mode.
  • Bank: roll together and decide when to bank the points. Bring your own dice if you prefer.
  • Random Event Dice: roll on a timer and track configured events.
  • Event Dice 2.0: configure dice, event rules, and session analytics.

Every page offers Light, Dark, and System appearance. Game board palettes are separate settings. The layouts put phone play first and also work on larger screens.

Look behind the moves

The 2048 lab compares 148 historical recorded runs across four strategies and two board sizes. Results, the inspectable run log, and the research library are separate views of the evidence.

Those historical runs come from a separate source app. They are observations, not a controlled benchmark of this browser's solver. The research and data notes explain the source, its limits, and the findings.
